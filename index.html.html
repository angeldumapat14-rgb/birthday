04 · My life journey

My story belongs to the Lord.

20

Every chapter of my life has shaped the person I am today. I have experienced challenges, changes, achievements, and lessons that have helped me become stronger. My journey has not always been easy, but I am grateful for every experience because it has taught me patience, courage, and perseverance.

One thing I will never forget is the moment I was won over by the Lord. I encountered Him during a G.E. gathering on April 12, 2025. I am incredibly thankful to Lotis because she was the one who invited me to church. That was where my true life began. When I decided to follow Jesus, the direction of my life changed because I allowed the Lord to take control of it.

I have learned so much and experienced tremendous growth. I became more aware, wiser, and more grounded in the truth. It was not always easy because the Lord removed certain people, things, and plans from my life that were not part of His will. However, I have learned that not everything we lose is meant to be part of our journey. Sometimes, letting go is part of God's purpose for us.

That is why I can say that this entire journey belongs to the Lord. He is the author of my story, and everything I have become is by His grace.
